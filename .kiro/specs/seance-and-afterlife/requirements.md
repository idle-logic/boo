# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The resurrection, finished. Before the old app is buried, the owner can hold a séance and talk to it. After the burial, the old app's ghost stays in the new app for 30 days and catches old habits as they are typed, so the mess never creeps back. That last part solves a real problem: after a switch, teams keep typing workarounds out of habit.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1

**User Story:** As a business owner, I want to talk to my old app before it goes, so that I understand why my data was such a mess.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the proof is shown THEN the system SHALL offer "Hold a séance" and "Lay Slotly to rest", and SHALL NOT bury the old app until the owner chooses.
2. WHEN the séance opens THEN the old app's ghost SHALL rise from the old app and speak first, naming what was hidden in it, from the findings.
3. WHEN the owner asks a question THEN the ghost SHALL answer in the first person, from its own records and its own limits, with real names and exact counts.
4. WHILE the séance is open THEN it SHALL show only the last few lines, never a scroll.
5. IF Gemini is busy THEN the ghost SHALL say the line is crackly and ask again.

### Requirement 2

**User Story:** As a business owner, I want the old app laid to rest when I say so, so that the switch feels finished.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the owner lays it to rest THEN the ghost SHALL say goodbye and sink back, and the old app SHALL be buried under a headstone with its name.

### Requirement 3

**User Story:** As a business owner, I want old habits caught in the new app, so that the clean data stays clean.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHILE the afterlife is on THEN every client or service saved in the new app SHALL be checked for old habits before it is stored.
2. WHEN a client is saved with marks in the name, a second number, credit or a package in the notes THEN the system SHALL store them in the proper fields and in Packages.
3. WHEN a service is saved as "RETAIL - ..." THEN the system SHALL store it as a product instead.
4. WHEN a habit is caught THEN the old app's ghost SHALL rise from its grave, fly to the fixed record, say "Old habits die hard. Trust me, I died.", list what it fixed, and go back to rest.
5. WHEN nothing needs fixing THEN the record SHALL be saved as typed, with no ghost.
6. WHEN the afterlife starts THEN the system SHALL offer to type an old habit so the owner can watch it being caught.
7. WHEN the data was raised from a file THEN the habits to try SHALL be built from the marks that file really used (its kind of VIP mark, alert, allergen, price with words), and the ghost SHALL catch them for that kind of record (clients, products, services or team).
