# Old habits

When you add or change how Boo reads data:

- Every hiding place needs its proper home: a mark in a name becomes a field, a note becomes a package or credit, a fake record becomes what it really is. See the `workaround-hunting` skill.
- Read by kind of record. People (clients), things sold (products, menu items), work done (services) and staff (team) each have their own marks.
- Keep the original text: anything without a field of its own goes in notes, never in the bin.
- Add a test with a real example from the samples and name its criterion. Then run `node bin/boo.mjs gate`.
