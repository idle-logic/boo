# Calm first

Haunt exists to take noise out of someone's day. Every screen must leave the person calmer and clearer than before. When in doubt, show less.

## Rules

- **One sentence tells the whole story.** The first thing anyone sees is a single plain sentence about how things stand, like "All good. 14 things handled since 9am."
- **Only what needs you.** The "Needs you" list holds real decisions only. Routine work never lands there. An empty list is the goal and is shown as a good thing.
- **Catch, do not add.** Notifications from the apps are caught by ghosts before they interrupt. Haunt never adds a notification of its own for something it already handled.
- **Workers are quiet.** Worker ghosts show what they are doing with motion, not words. Only Boo speaks, in one short line, and not often.
- **Details on demand.** Step-by-step logs, numbers and history stay folded away until someone asks.
- **No alarm colours for routine work.** Red and orange are only for things that need a person now.
- **Motion is gentle.** No flashing, no shaking unless something actually failed, and everything respects reduced motion.
- **Sound only when asked.** Boo talks out loud when spoken to or when something needs you, never for routine work.
