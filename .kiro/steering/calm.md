# Calm first

Switching software is stressful. Every screen must leave the person calmer and clearer than before. When in doubt, show less.

## Rules

- **One popup at a time,** floating over the apps, never a side panel and never a scroll.
- **The popup stays lit.** When something is highlighted, the dim goes behind the popup, not over it.
- **Waiting looks like waiting.** A spinner and one line say what Boo is doing; the Next button only appears when it is the person's turn.
- **Details on demand.** Lists of what moved fold away behind a toggle.
- **Workers are quiet.** Worker ghosts show what they are doing with motion, not words. Only Boo speaks, in one short line.
- **No alarm colours for routine work.** Red and orange are only for things that need a person now.
- **Motion is gentle.** No shaking unless something is being buried, and everything respects reduced motion.
