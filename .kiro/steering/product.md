# Haunt

Haunt finds the workarounds a team uses because its software cannot do what it needs, runs them as features inside that same software, and later switches the business to new software with nothing lost.

## The insight

Old software cannot hold everything, so teams improvise: packages in customer notes, allergies in names, products sold as fake services, clean-up blocks booked by hand, rules written as text. Each workaround is a missing feature done manually every day, and it is exactly what gets lost in a switch.

## What Haunt does

1. **Look around.** Boo goes through every screen of the old app, as a person would. No export, no API.
2. **Read between the lines.** Gemini reads names, notes and descriptions and says what they really mean. Every answer is checked against the original text; plain rules stand in when the AI is unavailable.
3. **Haunt it.** The workarounds become features Boo runs inside the old app: allergy guard, package tracker, auto clean-up, deposit nudge, VIP welcome.
4. **Switch.** Boo shows how each workaround becomes the new software's standard way and asks the owner to confirm, then moves everything and accounts for every record and every dollar.

## Principles

- **Calm.** One thing at a time on screen. Details are there if you open them.
- **Ask, don't guess.** Real choices go to the owner, with the original text beside the answer.
- **Grounded AI.** Nothing the AI says is used unless the original records back it up.
- **Prove it.** Done means every record accounted for, not that the typing stopped.
