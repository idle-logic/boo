# Haunt

Haunt moves a business from any old software to any new software, with nothing lost.

## The problem

Old software can't hold everything a business needs, so teams hide data where it doesn't belong: credit and prepaid passes in notes, allergies or "do not call" in names, products sold as fake services, blocked time booked as fake appointments, rules kept as a paragraph of text. Exports copy that mess straight into the new system. So switching means days of retyping, and things get lost.

## What Haunt does

1. **Read.** Boo goes through every screen of the old app, as a person would. No export, no API.
2. **Find.** Gemini reads names, notes and descriptions and works out what each workaround really is. Every answer is checked against the original text; plain rules stand in when the AI is unavailable.
3. **Clean.** Each workaround becomes the new app's proper field. The owner sees old → new side by side and can correct anything in plain words.
4. **Move and prove.** A swarm of ghosts enters everything through the new app's own screens, then every record and every dollar is reconciled.

The demo's example is a salon moving from "Slotly" (made up) to Idle. The same patterns appear in any software people outgrow: CRMs, booking, inventory, accounting, HR.

## Principles

- **Short and clear.** The demo plays by itself in about ninety seconds. One line of text at a time.
- **Ask, don't guess.** Real choices go to the owner, with the original text beside the answer.
- **Grounded AI.** Nothing the AI says is used unless the original records back it up.
- **Prove it.** Done means every record accounted for, not that the typing stopped.
