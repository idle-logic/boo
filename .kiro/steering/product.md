# Haunt

Haunt brings a business's data back from dying software, into any new software, with nothing lost.

## The problem

Old software can't hold everything a business needs, so teams hide data where it doesn't belong: credit and prepaid passes in notes, allergies or "do not call" in names, products sold as fake services, blocked time booked as fake appointments, rules kept as a paragraph of text. When the old app shuts down there is often no export at all, and an export would copy the mess anyway. So switching means days of retyping, and things get lost.

## The five phases

1. **Collect.** Boo reads every screen of the old app, as a person would. No export, no API.
2. **Analyse.** Rules and Gemini find each workaround. Gemini reads the notes no rule can read. Every AI answer is checked against the original text.
3. **Clean.** Each workaround becomes a proper field in the new app. The owner sees old → new side by side and corrects anything in plain words.
4. **Migrate.** A swarm of ghosts enters everything through the new app's own screens, all at once.
5. **Verify.** Every record and every dollar is reconciled.

Then the resurrection is finished: a **séance** with the old app before it goes, the **burial**, and the **afterlife**, where the old app's ghost keeps watch in the new app for 30 days and fixes old habits as they are typed.

**Raise anything.** Instead of the demo, anyone can drop their own dead data: a CSV export from any software, a screenshot, or a photo of a paper book.

The demo is a salon moving from "Slotly" (made up, shutting down) to Idle. The same patterns appear in any software people outgrow: CRMs, booking, inventory, accounting, HR.

## Principles

- **The person is in charge.** Every phase waits for Next. Boo asks what matters before it starts.
- **Boo can always be asked.** Tap Boo at any moment to ask about the data or change anything.
- **Grounded AI.** Nothing the AI says is used unless the original records back it up, and Boo never claims a change it did not make.
- **One thing at a time.** One popup, one line of text, never a scroll.
- **Prove it.** Done means every record accounted for, not that the typing stopped.
