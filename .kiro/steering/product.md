# Haunt

Haunt puts Boo, a small pixel ghost, on your screen. Boo walks along your taskbar, hops between the tops of your windows and keeps an eye on Kiro while you look away.

## Who it is for

Developers who hand long tasks to an AI agent and then switch to something else. The agent finishes, or stops to wait for an approval, and nobody notices for twenty minutes.

## The dead tech we bring back

Desktop pets from the 1990s: eSheep, the walking cats, the paperclip. They were charming and useless. Boo is charming and useful.

## What Boo does

- **Kiro starts a task:** Boo picks up a lantern and walks about busily, with the task in a speech bubble.
- **Kiro runs a tool:** Boo says what it is running, briefly.
- **Kiro needs you:** Boo flies to your cursor and knocks on the glass until you click it.
- **Kiro finishes:** Boo jumps for joy, with confetti.
- **Something fails:** Boo turns pale and shivers.
- **Nothing happening:** Boo wanders, naps on a window and can be picked up and thrown.

## Principles

- **Never in the way.** Every click goes through to your apps unless you click Boo.
- **Never fails a hook.** The CLI and MCP tools exit quietly when Haunt is not running.
- **Waiting on you comes first.** Nothing replaces "Kiro needs you" until you click.
- **Nothing leaves the machine.** Events go to 127.0.0.1 only.
